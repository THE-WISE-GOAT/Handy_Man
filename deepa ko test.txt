Once upon a time, a curious rabbit and a steady turtle decided to race through the forest.
The rabbit darted ahead with bursts of speed, laughing at how slowly the turtle moved.
The turtle kept going, one careful step at a time, never losing faith in his steady pace.
When the rabbit grew tired and decided to rest, the turtle passed by quietly and continued onward.
By the time the rabbit woke up, the turtle had already crossed the finish line with calm determination.
In the end, the rabbit learned that speed is useful, but persistence and consistency win the day.
