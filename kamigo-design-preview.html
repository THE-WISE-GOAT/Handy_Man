01 · Palette

Six colors, one accent

Orange is the only chromatic color in the system. Job state is encoded as orange-filled vs. gray-hollow rather than green/blue — that removes the off-brand #4ade80 green and #3b82f6 blue currently in your buttons and chat bubbles. One red survives, reserved strictly for the emergency toggle.

04 · Cards

Job cards with a status rail

The signature element: a rail on the left edge whose fill height encodes how far the job has moved through dispatch — posted, matched, assigned, done. It carries real information rather than decorating. In your current build these cards render as white boxes with black borders on the charcoal page, which is the most jarring thing in the UI.
